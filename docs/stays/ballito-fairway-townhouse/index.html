A bright townhouse looking out over several fairways. On the ground floor, open-plan living, dining and kitchen areas lead out to a covered patio, a wooden deck and a private pool.

Upstairs are three bedrooms: the main suite has the best of the fairway views. It sleeps six, with a cot and high chair, lake access and free resort access.
