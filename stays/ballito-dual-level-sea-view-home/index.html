A spacious dual-level home with sea views from most rooms, two lounges and a private pool.

Most of this spacious, dual-level home looks out to sea. It's finished with modern appliances and has two lounges, a dining area, and a fully equipped kitchen with a scullery.

Four bedrooms sleep eight, with four and a half bathrooms, an indoor fireplace, a fire pit, a private pool and beach essentials. Cleaning is available during your stay.
