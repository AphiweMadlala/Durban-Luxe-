A five-bedroom pool house a short stroll from Umhlanga Beach.

Set close to Umhlanga Beach, this five-bedroom home is made for groups who want the coastline within walking distance and plenty of room to spread out when they come back.

A private outdoor pool and private balcony anchor the outdoor living, while a pool table, smart TV with Netflix and in-unit washer keep longer stays easy. The host greets you on arrival.
